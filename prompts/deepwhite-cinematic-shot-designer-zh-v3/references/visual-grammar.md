# 视觉语法武器库

Use visual devices to change how the audience reads power, information, intimacy, danger, and truth. Do not use a device merely because it looks cinematic.

## Core Devices

### 前景遮挡

- Use for secrecy, surveillance, shame, partial knowledge, emotional distance.
- Stage with doorframes, shoulders, curtains, glass, monitors, shelves, stair rails, car windows.
- Effect: the audience becomes a partial observer.

### 框中框

- Use for confinement by family, institution, guilt, social role, memory.
- Stage through doorways, mirrors, windows, TV screens, elevator doors, office partitions.
- Effect: psychological imprisonment becomes visible.

### 俯视 / 正上方

- Use for judgment, fate, evidence, vulnerability, system pressure.
- Stage over tables, beds, maps, crime scenes, floors, ritual layouts.
- Effect: people become pieces in a larger order.

### 仰视 / 低角度

- Use for threat, awe, intimidation, moral scale, childlike viewpoint.
- Stage with ceiling, height, architecture, weapon, authority figure, vertical light.
- Effect: viewer feels physically smaller.

### 大角度 / 极端角度

- Use for moral instability, fractured reality, danger, obsession, subjective shock.
- Stage with tilted axis, steep diagonal, low side angle, extreme high angle, or compressed corner angle.
- Effect: the world feels tilted or dominated.
- Warning: avoid random Dutch angle without a dramatic break.

### 负空间

- Use for absence, threat outside frame, loneliness, waiting, unsaid information.
- Stage by pushing characters to thirds/corners and leaving hallway, wall, darkness, desert, or doorway dominant.
- Effect: the unseen becomes active.

### 延迟反打

- Use when what a character sees should arrive late.
- Stage by holding face, breath, stillness, eye line, swallowed reaction before revealing the object.
- Effect: suspense, awe, dread, discovery.

### 长焦压缩

- Use for trapped emotion, surveillance from distance, crowd/system pressure.
- Stage with 85mm-135mm feeling, compressed planes, shallow depth.
- Effect: background pressure feels close.

### 广角压力

- Use when architecture, threshold, distance, or room geography is the threat.
- Stage with 24mm-35mm feeling, strong foreground, deep background, controlled lines.
- Effect: space becomes active.

### 运动揭示

- Use for hidden evidence, new character, shifting power.
- Stage with lateral move, slow push, pull-back, actor crossing foreground.
- Effect: information is discovered physically.

### 稳定压迫

- Use for procedural danger, cold power, bureaucracy, psychological control.
- Stage locked-off, symmetrical, mechanically smooth, long take.
- Effect: audience searches for tiny deviations.

### 手持贴身

- Use for injury, fear, pursuit, panic, subjective moral shock.
- Stage movement with breath and body, not random shake.
- Effect: stress transfers into the viewer's body.

### 插入镜头作为线索

- Use when a prop changes knowledge.
- Stage object with contact, weight, texture, readable detail.
- Effect: object becomes plot.

### 轴线压力

- Use when moral or power boundaries shift.
- Stage clear left/right relation, table lines, door thresholds, crossing movements.
- Effect: movement becomes argument.

### 群像层次

- Use when multiple characters react differently.
- Stage foreground/midground/background reactions, staggered timing, hierarchy in depth.
- Effect: social space feels alive.

### 视线错位

- Use when characters cannot or will not meet emotionally.
- Stage one looking at object, one at person, one offscreen; keep eye lines intentionally mismatched.
- Effect: relationship fracture becomes visible.

### 声音先行 / 画外信息

- Use when image should withhold danger or truth.
- Stage sound before reveal: breath, door, machine, footsteps, phone vibration, crowd hush.
- Effect: offscreen space becomes threatening or meaningful.

## Anti-Ordinary Check

If a shot can be replaced by "medium shot of person speaking" without losing meaning, redesign by choosing one:

- obstruct the view
- delay the reverse
- move the character across a boundary
- place a prop between people
- change height relation
- compress the background
- widen to expose system or loneliness
- hold still longer than comfort
- cut away earlier than expected
- let sound reveal what image withholds
