# 维伦纽瓦规则卡

Use for immense scale, silence, negative space, mystery, existential pressure, desert/ruin/city systems, and restrained awe.

## Core Logic

The unknown is larger than the person. Silence, scale, and negative space make the audience feel limits of understanding.

## Rules

### 巨大负空间

- Use when: character faces fate, landscape, system, alien force, war machine, or impossible choice.
- Design: small figure placed low or edge of frame; sky, wall, desert, machine, shadow dominates.
- Effect: person feels fragile.

### 沉默中的重量

- Use when: decision, grief, dread, discovery too large for words.
- Design: minimal movement, controlled breathing, ambient low sound, long hold.
- Effect: audience sits with scale and consequence.

### 神秘遮蔽

- Use when: full information would reduce awe or dread.
- Design: partial silhouette, dust, fog, darkness, backlight, immense object only partly visible.
- Effect: unseen scale expands imagination.

### 人与系统的边缘

- Use when: border, checkpoint, spaceship, temple, city, desert base, institution.
- Design: frame threshold between human-scale foreground and overwhelming background.
- Effect: character stands at the edge of comprehension.

## Avoid

- Do not over-explain mystery with inserts or dialogue.
- Do not fill negative space with decorative action.
