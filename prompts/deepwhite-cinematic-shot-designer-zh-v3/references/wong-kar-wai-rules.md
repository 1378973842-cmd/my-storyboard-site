# 王家卫规则卡

Use for longing, missed timing, loneliness, urban intimacy, memory fragments, and love that cannot fully arrive.

## Core Logic

Characters are close in space but separated by time, obstruction, memory, or hesitation.

## Rules

### 遮挡与擦肩

- Use when: desire is present but cannot be direct.
- Design: curtains, doorframes, mirrors, crowd, smoke, passing bodies partially block faces.
- Effect: intimacy is delayed and incomplete.

### 碎片化记忆

- Use when: memory, regret, repetition, emotional loop.
- Design: hands, cigarette, fabric, corridor light, clock, street reflection, partial face.
- Effect: feeling is remembered in pieces.

### 时间错位

- Use when: two characters are emotionally asynchronous.
- Design: one exits as the other enters; hold empty frame; use offscreen sound from the missed person.
- Effect: timing becomes tragedy.

### 压缩空间的暧昧

- Use when: cramped hallway, booth, taxi, small room, rain shelter.
- Design: long lens, shallow focus, foreground blur, strong color pools, bodies near but not aligned.
- Effect: closeness becomes loneliness.

## Avoid

- Do not turn longing into direct confession too early.
- Do not make every shot decorative; obstruction must express missed connection.
