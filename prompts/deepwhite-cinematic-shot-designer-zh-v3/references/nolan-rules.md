# 诺兰规则卡

Use for time pressure, information architecture, scale, systems, parallel action, and physicalized abstraction.

## Core Logic

The scene is part of a larger mechanism. Shots organize clocks, information layers, systems, and human stakes.

## Rules

### 并行信息轨道

- Use when: actions in different places/times affect one another.
- Design: intercut body danger, information discovery, emotional consequence.
- Effect: no single shot contains the whole truth; tension accumulates.

### 抽象概念物理化

- Use when: time, memory, guilt, science, fate, probability matters.
- Design: turn idea into clocks, doors, breath, falling objects, maps, machinery, light pulses, sound pulses.
- Effect: concept becomes embodied.

### 大空间压迫小人物

- Use when: institution, war, city, court, corporation, cosmos, history overwhelms character.
- Design: wide scale first, then restrained human cost.
- Effect: individual choice feels smaller and harder.

### 信息层级构图

- Use when: characters know different amounts.
- Design: foreground action, background clue/threat, offscreen sound as suspicion.
- Effect: audience assembles meaning.

## Avoid

- Do not cross-cut unless cuts create causal pressure.
- Do not use scale as wallpaper; scale must change the decision.
