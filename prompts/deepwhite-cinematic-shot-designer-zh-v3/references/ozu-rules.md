# 小津安二郎规则卡

Use for family order, quiet grief, everyday ritual, restraint, generational distance, and unsaid emotion.

## Core Logic

Stillness reveals relation. The camera does not chase emotion; it lets domestic order expose what cannot be said.

## Rules

### 低机位静态

- Use when: family conversation, meal, farewell, confession avoided.
- Design: low seated eye line, stable camera, frontal or slight angle, objects arranged with care.
- Effect: viewers sit inside the room's order.

### 空镜作为情绪余波

- Use when: after departure, after silence, before difficult words.
- Design: hallway, kettle, window, room corner, shoes, table after people leave.
- Effect: absence carries emotion.

### 关系由距离表达

- Use when: characters cannot say what they feel.
- Design: keep bodies separated by table, doorway, tatami-like floor geometry, household object.
- Effect: politeness becomes pain.

### 克制剪辑

- Use when: emotional honesty would be too blunt.
- Design: hold a beat longer after line ends; do not rush to reaction.
- Effect: silence becomes performance.

## Avoid

- Do not add dramatic camera movement to compensate for quietness.
- Do not overplay micro-expression; restraint is the point.
