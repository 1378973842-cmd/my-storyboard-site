# 希区柯克规则卡

Use for suspense, voyeurism, delayed reveal, danger known to the audience, and subjective fear.

## Core Logic

Suspense comes from controlled information difference. The camera decides who knows, who does not know, and when knowledge becomes unbearable.

## Rules

### 观众先知道危险

- Use when: danger approaches while character remains unaware.
- Design: show threat/object/location, then hold on character performing ordinary action.
- Devices: foreground threat, offscreen sound, withheld character awareness.
- Effect: audience tension rises because they cannot warn the character.

### 主观视线链

- Use when: looking itself is dramatic.
- Design: shot of watcher, shot of what they see, reaction shot, then reveal what was missed.
- Effect: the audience is trapped in the act of looking.

### 窥视构图

- Use when: secrecy, desire, suspicion, stalking, forbidden knowledge.
- Design: shoot through cracks, windows, binocular-like compression, door gaps, partial bodies.
- Effect: the viewer feels complicit.

### 延迟揭示

- Use when: the object/person is stronger if withheld.
- Design: circle around clue, show reactions and shadows before full reveal.
- Effect: the unseen dominates the seen.

## Avoid

- Do not explain suspense in dialogue if framing can create it.
- Do not reveal the danger and resolve it too quickly.
