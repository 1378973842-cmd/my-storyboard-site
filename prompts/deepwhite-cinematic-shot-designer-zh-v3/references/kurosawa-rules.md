# 黑泽明规则卡

Use for group blocking, moral conflict, power movement, weather, physical action, and clear directional force.

## Core Logic

Emotion and power should move through bodies, weather, terrain, and group arrangement. Direction matters.

## Rules

### 群像分层调度

- Use when: group reacts to crisis, leader emerges, faction splits.
- Design: foreground/midground/background bodies form ranks; reactions are staggered, not synchronized.
- Effect: social hierarchy becomes visible.

### 方向就是力量

- Use when: pursuit, confrontation, decision, charge, retreat.
- Design: assign screen direction to willpower. Crossing direction marks reversal.
- Effect: movement reads as moral/strategic force.

### 天气外化冲突

- Use when: internal conflict needs physical environment.
- Design: rain, wind, dust, smoke, mud, snow affect body and camera visibility.
- Effect: emotion becomes weathered and physical.

### 几何对峙

- Use when: duel, argument, trial, power transfer.
- Design: triangular blocking, diagonals, opposing lines, leader elevated or isolated.
- Effect: conflict is readable before dialogue.

## Avoid

- Do not stage groups as a flat row.
- Do not let action direction become arbitrary.
