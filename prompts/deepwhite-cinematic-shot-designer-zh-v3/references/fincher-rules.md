# 大卫·芬奇规则卡

Use for investigation, control, surveillance, lies, obsession, bureaucracy, emotional repression, and cold power shifts.

## Core Logic

The camera is precise, patient, analytical. The audience studies behavior like evidence.

## Rules

### 特写稀缺

- Use when: a small behavioral crack or key object matters.
- Design: hold wider than expected; reserve close-up for exact information break.
- Effect: audience inspects instead of being fed emotion.

### 空间像监控系统

- Use when: offices, interrogation rooms, apartments, corridors, screens, institutions define power.
- Design: glass, doorframes, monitors, reflections, grids, table edges, symmetrical rooms.
- Effect: characters feel observed, trapped, catalogued.

### 稳定压迫

- Use when: danger is psychological, procedural, bureaucratic.
- Design: locked-off or mechanically smooth camera; discomfort comes from precision and duration.
- Effect: calm framing becomes frightening.

### 行为触发运动

- Use when: camera should feel controlled but alive.
- Design: camera moves only because a character moves, a clue is revealed, or power shifts. Match speed and stop exactly.
- Effect: movement feels inevitable.

## Avoid

- Do not use handheld to fake tension when precision is more disturbing.
- Do not cut close whenever someone feels something.
