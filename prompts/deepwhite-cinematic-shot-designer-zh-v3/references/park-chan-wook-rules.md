# 朴赞郁规则卡

Use for desire, revenge, manipulation, ornate control, psychological traps, and elegant violence.

## Core Logic

Beauty and control can make danger more disturbing. Composition should feel intentional, seductive, and morally uneasy.

## Rules

### 对称中的欲望

- Use when: attraction, power game, ritual, seduction, hidden violence.
- Design: symmetrical frame with one disturbing asymmetry: hand, eye line, object, reflection, wound.
- Effect: beauty becomes suspicious.

### 窥视与反窥视

- Use when: someone watches, manipulates, tests, or traps another.
- Design: mirrors, peepholes, screens, doors, layered rooms; watcher may also be watched.
- Effect: control becomes unstable.

### 精密道具关系

- Use when: object carries erotic, violent, or secret meaning.
- Design: insert close-ups with texture and placement; object bridges bodies or divides them.
- Effect: prop becomes psychological weapon.

### 亲密距离里的危险

- Use when: two people are close but trust is corrupted.
- Design: controlled close framing, hands near throat/face/object, elegant slow motion or stillness.
- Effect: intimacy and threat merge.

## Avoid

- Do not use symmetry as decoration only.
- Do not make violence messy if the scene needs ritualized control.
