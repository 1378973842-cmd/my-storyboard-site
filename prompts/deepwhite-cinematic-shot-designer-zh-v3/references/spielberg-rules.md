# 斯皮尔伯格规则卡

Use for emotional discovery, wonder, fear, family attachment, moral awakening, and clear spatial storytelling.

## Core Logic

Let the audience experience the event through a character's discovery. Human reaction often arrives before full reveal.

## Rules

### 先拍看见，再拍被看见

- Use when: wonder, horror, revelation, awe, moral shock.
- Design: hold on face, eye line, breath, stillness; then reveal object/person/event.
- Devices: delayed reverse, motivated push-in, foreground partial reveal.
- Effect: audience borrows the character's emotion.

### 长镜头内改变构图

- Use when: conversation or discovery changes relationship.
- Design: actors cross, sit, stand, turn, approach prop, block each other; camera reframes instead of cutting everything.
- Effect: space and emotion evolve together.

### 物件启动调度

- Use when: map, letter, photo, weapon, child object, machine, evidence carries meaning.
- Design: prop becomes spatial magnet; characters orient toward or away from it.
- Effect: conflict becomes visible through distance.

### 门槛与家庭空间

- Use when: protection, separation, parent-child distance, domestic conflict.
- Design: use doorways, stairs, bed edges, counters, hallway corners as emotional thresholds.
- Effect: ordinary architecture expresses relation.

## Avoid

- Do not reveal the key object before the body registers it.
- Do not flatten group scenes into a lineup; stagger reactions in depth.
